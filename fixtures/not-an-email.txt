This is a plain text file with no email structure whatsoever -- no From
line, no To line, no Subject, no header block of any kind, just prose.

It exists to prove that audit.py refuses to produce a report against
input that was never an email in the first place, rather than silently
treating everything as blank/missing and generating a full-looking
report anyway. See NotAnEmailError in audit.py and the "Non-email input"
row in fixtures/manifest.md.
